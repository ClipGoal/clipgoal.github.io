1. What ClipForge is

ClipForge is a private desktop application operated by ClipGoal. It runs only on the operator's own computer and is used solely to upload short clips to the operator's own YouTube channels ClipGoal (@clipgoal) and ClipGoal World (@clipgoalworld). ClipForge is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else, and there are no user accounts for third parties.

2. YouTube Terms of Service

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services. By using ClipForge or watching our channels you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information about how Google handles data is in the Google Privacy Policy. How ClipForge handles data is described in our Privacy Policy.

3. Content and credit

Clips are made only from public streams of streamers whose clipping permission was checked. Every video names the original streamer and links to their channel. Clips with copyrighted music, third-party videos or sensitive content are blocked before upload.

4. Removal requests

If you are a streamer or rights holder and want a clip removed, write to clip.goal@gmx.de. We remove it as quickly as possible.

5. Revoking access

Google account access for ClipForge can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. No warranty

ClipForge and this website are provided as they are, without any warranty. Contact: clip.goal@gmx.de
